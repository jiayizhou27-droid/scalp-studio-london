What scalp micropigmentation does, how the effect is built, and what to consider before choosing it.

SCALP MICROPIGMENTATION · SMP

The appearance of density.

SMP uses fine needles to place small pigment impressions in the scalp. These impressions can reduce the contrast between visible scalp and existing hair, or create a closely shaved appearance.

It is a cosmetic form of tattooing. It does not transplant follicles, grow new strands or treat the cause of hair loss.

PIGMENTS, PRACTICE & PEACE OF MIND

Know what goes
into your treatment.

Reassurance should come from clear product information and careful practice, rather than a promise that any pigment is completely harmless.

01

Understand the pigment.

Micropigmentation inks contain colour particles in a liquid carrier. Ingredients vary by product. The exact brand, shade, ingredient information and manufacturer documentation should be available before treatment.

Studio product details awaiting confirmation.

02

Ask about traceability.

Ask to see the product label, batch number, expiry date and relevant safety information. Terms such as “natural”, “organic” or “vegan” do not, by themselves, establish that a product cannot cause a reaction.

03

Look beyond the bottle.

Safe practice also depends on infection control, sterile single-use needles, appropriate handling and written aftercare. Ask your practitioner to explain the procedure and the precautions used.

Informed, not overpromised.

Like other skin-piercing pigment procedures, SMP can cause allergic reactions, infection, scarring or an unwanted cosmetic result. Discuss allergies, medicines and existing scalp conditions before deciding. A patch test, where indicated, cannot rule out every immediate or future reaction.

Following treatment, seek medical advice for worsening redness, swelling, discharge, severe pain or fever. Follow the individual aftercare instructions provided by your practitioner.

A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Your questions,
considered.

Is this suitable for women with long hair?

Density work may reduce visible scalp between existing hairs while retaining a longer hairstyle. Suitability depends on your existing coverage, scalp and desired result, and is assessed individually.

Will I need to shave my hair?

Not every treatment involves a shaved look. Longer-hair density and closely shaved styles are different plans. We discuss any hair-length requirements before you book an operation course.

How is my treatment area measured?

Measurements follow the scalp surface. A regular area is estimated by length × width; curved or irregular regions are split into smaller sections. The final boundary includes agreed blending work and is confirmed in person.

What is included in the operation price?

The proposed rate covers three agreed operation sessions, consumables and written aftercare. Assessment and standalone design are charged separately. Any additional sessions or expanded coverage must be quoted before booking.

Are consultations free or deductible?

No. Assessment and design are paid professional appointments. Their fees are separate from, and are not deducted from, the operation fee.
